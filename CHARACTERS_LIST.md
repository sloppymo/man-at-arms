# Characters in Man-at-Arms Game

## Main Companions (Relationship Tracked NPCs)

1. **Wat** - A veteran soldier, cynical and practical. Known for his knife and dark humor. Focus of several campfire vignettes.

2. **Cook** - The camp cook, formerly an apprentice in a noble's kitchen. Precise, methodical, cares about food quality. Focus of several campfire vignettes.

3. **Oana** - A companion with relationship tracking. (Appears in relationship system but less detail found in scenes)

## Patrons (Selectable at Character Creation)

4. **Sir James "The Reaver" de Looney** - Free Company captain, wildcard, known for murder and pillage. Profitable but unstable.

5. **Sir David de Montfort** - Noble Household, timid but fair third son. Values his men's lives. Safer, slower path.

6. **Baron Caley of Tournai** - Noble Household, indifferent to your life. Higher glory and plunder if you survive.

7. **Count Charles "The Grim" of Suffolk** - Noble Household, grizzled leader, drinking hard. Veterans, volatility, hard lessons.

8. **Ashkhan of the Mamluk Guard** - Mercenary Company, respected mercenary from the Levant. Tactical expertise, professional and disciplined.

## Named NPCs (Companions and Camp Followers)

9. **Porridge Tom** - Drunkard who sings marching songs badly. Appears in random encounters.

10. **Fiddle Jack** - A bowman/fiddler. Appears in various scenes, often with his fiddle.

11. **Soot-Eyed Billy** - A soldier, appears in multiple battle scenes. Observant, gives tactical advice.

12. **Hammer-hand Harry** - A blacksmith or craftsman with strong hands. Appears in gambling debt scene.

13. **Rat-Catcher Ned** - Tells tall tales around the campfire. Known for stories about geese and French knights.

14. **Hoary Bill** - A soldier who appears in the lost boot encounter. Grins, gives advice.

15. **Sixpence Tom** - A bowman with a dice cup. Gambler who collects debts.

16. **Granny Six-Teeth** - An older woman who helps with baggage and supplies. Sharp-tongued, appears in multiple battle scenes giving practical advice.

17. **Lloyd the Welshman** - A Welsh soldier. Appears in the stolen horse encounter. Philosophical, makes observations.

## Supporting Characters

18. **Marie** - The blacksmith's daughter. A romantic interest option. Pretty, brings food. Can court her, but may be a cruel joke.

19. **The Blacksmith** - Marie's father. Big man, hard hands. Appears in forge scenes and blacksmith shop scenes.

20. **Geoffrey** - A translator. Appears in scenes where translation is needed (likely for French interactions).

21. **The Old Man** - A French peasant who appears in scenes asking for help. Has a sick daughter, lost his wife.

## Notes

- The player character is customizable (name, age, origin, culture)
- Many characters are referred to by descriptive nicknames rather than full names
- Some characters appear only in specific scenes or random encounters
- Wat, Cook, and Oana are the main relationship-tracked companions
- The patrons are major figures who determine the player's path through the game
