## Style Characteristics Breakdown

### Visual Style Elements:
- **Art Form**: 16-bit pixel art, classic retro video game aesthetic
- **Visual Style**: Visible square pixels, strong black outlines defining all elements
- **Border**: Thick, dark brown or reddish-brown border frames the entire image, giving it the appearance of a framed artwork (sometimes with a small decorative symbol in corner)
- **Period Reference**: Classic SNES/Genesis RPGs (Chrono Trigger, Final Fantasy VI, Shining Force, Secret of Mana)
- **Color Palette**: Rich earthy tones - dark browns, greys, deep greens, warm oranges and yellows from fires, metallic greys and silvers for armor, deep blues and purples for night skies
- **Perspective**: Flat, isometric or side-view typical of 16-bit RPGs
- **Figures**: Pixelated with clear black outlines, expressive pixel faces, dynamic poses
- **Details**: Detailed pixel art despite pixelation - shows armor details, weapons, facial expressions, textures
- **Lighting**: Atmospheric dramatic lighting with strong contrasts (warm firelight vs. cool shadows)
- **Mood**: Gritty, realistic medieval atmosphere despite pixel art style

### Historical Accuracy:
- **Period**: Hundred Years' War (1337-1453)
- **Armor**: Plate armor, chainmail, bascinets, kettle hats
- **Weapons**: Longbows, swords, lances, spears, daggers, axes
- **Architecture**: Castles, fortified towns, stone buildings, timber-framed houses
- **Clothing**: Tunics, surcoats, hose, cloaks, hoods - period-accurate 14th century
- **Heraldry**: Shields with fleurs-de-lis, lions, castles, heraldic banners

### Mood & Atmosphere:
- **Tone**: Gritty, atmospheric, visceral (McCarthy-esque)
- **Action**: Dynamic, chaotic battle scenes, tense confrontations
- **Quiet Moments**: Camp life, rest, preparation, contemplation
- **Weather**: Rain, storms, fog, harsh conditions
- **Emotion**: Determination, exhaustion, camaraderie, fear, resolve
