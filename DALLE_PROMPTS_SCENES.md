**Title**: Campfire - Maintenance  
**Setting**: Brief pause during march, column resting  
**Mood**: Quiet, focused, routine maintenance by firelight

```
16-bit pixel art, medieval Hundred Years' War camp scene at dusk or night. Three soldiers seated around a stone-ringed campfire, maintaining their gear by firelight. One soldier sharpening a long knife or short sword with a whetstone, holding the blade in both hands. Another mending a piece of white cloth with a needle. A third working on leather straps or a pouch. Visible square pixels, strong black outlines defining all elements. Rich earthy color palette: dark browns, greys, deep greens for the ground and tents, warm oranges and yellows from the campfire flames casting dynamic light and shadows. Dark purple-blue twilight sky transitioning to darker blue at top, dotted with faint stars. Sparks and embers rising from the fire. Several conical medieval tents visible in background. Camp items scattered around: helmet, shield, tools, leather scraps, metal cup. Textured ground with dirt, small stones, sparse grass. Framed by a thick, dark brown or reddish-brown border around the entire image, giving it the appearance of a framed artwork. Classic retro video game aesthetic, reminiscent of SNES or Genesis RPGs. Detailed pixel art despite pixelation. Atmospheric dramatic lighting with strong contrast between warm firelight and cool night tones. Period-accurate 14th century armor, weapons, camp equipment. Gritty, realistic medieval atmosphere. High quality pixel art, 16-bit era style.
```

## Pixel Art Style Characteristics

**Key Style Elements:**
- **Art Form**: 16-bit pixel art, classic retro video game aesthetic
- **Visual Style**: Visible square pixels, strong black outlines defining all elements
- **Border**: Thick, dark brown or reddish-brown border frames the entire image, giving it the appearance of a framed artwork (sometimes with a small decorative symbol in corner)
- **Color Palette**: Rich earthy tones - dark browns, greys, deep greens, warm oranges and yellows from fires, metallic greys for armor
- **Lighting**: Atmospheric dramatic lighting with strong contrasts (warm firelight vs. cool shadows)
- **Detail**: Detailed pixel art despite pixelation - shows armor, weapons, expressions, textures
- **Mood**: Gritty, realistic medieval atmosphere
- **Reference**: Similar to classic SNES or Genesis RPGs (Chrono Trigger, Final Fantasy VI, Shining Force)
