# Patron Character Descriptions for Image Generation

## 1. Sir James "The Reaver" de Looney

**Description:**
A dangerous-looking man in his early thirties, with a wild, unpredictable energy. Medium build but wiry and quick. Dark, unkempt hair that falls past his shoulders, often greasy from lack of care. Sharp, calculating eyes that never seem to fully rest—always scanning, assessing. A few scars visible on his face and hands, souvenirs from his violent profession. 

Wears expensive but carelessly maintained clothing: a fine wool tunic in dark burgundy or deep green, now stained and worn at the edges. Rich fabric that shows he profits well from his raids, but the disheveled state reveals his chaotic nature. Leather boots scuffed and mud-caked. A heavy belt with a large buckle, perhaps silver or brass, tarnished. No jewelry except maybe a single ring on a finger, something taken from a victim. His posture is slightly hunched, ready to spring, like a predator at rest. A faint, unsettling smile that doesn't reach his eyes.

**Setting:** Standing in a camp or tavern, surrounded by rough-looking men. Background suggests wealth but disorder—fine tents or furnishings mixed with chaos.

## 2. Sir David de Montfort

**Description:**
A young nobleman in his mid-twenties, with a gentle but anxious demeanor. Fair, well-groomed hair cut in the fashion of a third son—presentable but not ostentatious. Soft features that haven't yet hardened from war. Kind eyes that show worry and care for others. Clean-shaven or with a light, carefully maintained beard. Slender build, not yet filled out with the muscle of a veteran warrior.

Wears well-made but modest noble clothing: a clean tunic in soft blue or gray wool, properly maintained. A simple surcoat, perhaps with a small family crest embroidered on it. Clothes are neat and cared for, showing his careful nature. Soft leather boots, well-polished. His hands are clean, perhaps with ink stains from reading or correspondence. He stands with a slight uncertainty, as if always second-guessing himself. A book or scroll might be visible nearby, showing his educated background.

**Setting:** In a clean, organized tent or study. Orderly surroundings that reflect his methodical nature. Perhaps a map or documents on a table.

## 3. Baron Caley of Tournai

**Description:**
A middle-aged nobleman in his forties, with an air of cold indifference. Well-built and imposing, but his expression shows he's seen you as a number, not a person. Dark hair graying at the temples, kept short and practical. Sharp, assessing eyes that look through you rather than at you. A strong jaw and prominent features that speak of authority and privilege. Well-maintained appearance, but no warmth in his face.

Wears expensive, well-tailored clothing that shows his status: a rich tunic in deep purple or black, made of fine fabric. A surcoat with his heraldry clearly displayed—he wants you to know who he is. Everything is clean, pressed, and maintained by servants. Fine leather boots, polished to a shine. Perhaps a signet ring on his finger. His posture is straight, formal, distant. He might be holding a goblet of wine or reviewing papers, showing his focus is on business, not people.

**Setting:** In a well-appointed tent or hall. Rich furnishings, maps on tables, symbols of power and wealth. Everything organized and efficient, but impersonal.

## 4. Count Charles "The Grim" of Suffolk

**Description:**
A grizzled veteran in his late forties or early fifties, with a hard-drinking, battle-worn appearance. Gray-streaked dark hair, kept short but unkempt. A full beard, gray and rough. Deep-set eyes that have seen too much, surrounded by lines and shadows. A prominent nose, perhaps broken once. Scars visible on his face and hands. His expression is grim and weary, but with an underlying volatility—the kind of man who could snap at any moment.

Wears good-quality but well-worn clothing: a tunic in dark colors (brown, gray, or deep red), showing signs of use and age. The fabric is good but not pristine—a man who's been at war for decades. Leather that's been patched. A heavy cloak, perhaps. His hands are calloused and scarred. He might be holding a tankard of ale or wine, or have one nearby. His posture suggests both strength and exhaustion. There's a dangerous edge to him, like a blade that's been used too much but is still sharp.

**Setting:** In a veteran's tent or camp area. Maps, weapons, and the detritus of long campaigns visible. A sense of hard-won experience and accumulated weariness.

## 5. Ashkhan of the Mamluk Guard

**Description:**
A professional mercenary in his thirties, with the disciplined bearing of a career soldier from the Levant. Dark hair, kept short and practical. Dark eyes that are sharp and observant, showing tactical intelligence. Olive or tan skin. Clean-shaven or with a well-maintained, short beard. Athletic build—agile and strong, the result of professional training. His expression is calm, controlled, professional—no wasted emotion.

Wears practical, well-maintained clothing that blends Eastern and Western styles: a tunic in earth tones (brown, tan, or deep green), well-fitted and functional. Perhaps some elements of Middle Eastern dress—a turban or head wrap, or distinctive patterns in the fabric. Everything is clean and organized, showing discipline. Good-quality leather boots and belt. His posture is straight, military, efficient. He might be studying a map or tactical diagram, showing his strategic mind. No unnecessary ornamentation—everything serves a purpose.

**Setting:** In a well-organized tent or command area. Maps, tactical diagrams, and organized equipment visible. Everything in its place, reflecting his professional, disciplined nature.

## Notes for Image Generation:

- All descriptions are for **non-battle clothing**—civilian or camp attire
- Time period: 14th century (1337-1356, Hundred Years' War era)
- Setting: English/French conflict, medieval Europe
- Focus on personality and status reflected in clothing and appearance
- Each patron should look distinct and memorable
- Consider the game's dark, realistic tone—not romanticized medieval fantasy
